====================================================
GOOGLE ANALYTICS SETUP GUIDE FOR WANDER
====================================================

WHAT IS THIS?
-------------
Google Analytics (GA4) tracks who visits your website, what pages they view,
how long they stay, and where they come from. This data helps you understand
your audience and improve your site.

The setup has 2 parts:
1. Creating a Google Analytics account (done on Google's website)
2. Adding your unique tracking ID to your project (explained below)


STEP 1: CREATE A GOOGLE ANALYTICS ACCOUNT
-----------------------------------------
1. Go to: https://analytics.google.com/

2. Sign in with your Google account

3. Click "Start measuring" (or "Create Account" if you already have GA)

4. Fill in the account details:
   - Account name: "Wander" (or whatever you want to call it)
   - Click Next

5. Create a Property:
   - Property name: "Wander Website"
   - Select your timezone and currency
   - Click Next

6. Fill in business details:
   - Industry: Travel
   - Business size: Small
   - Click Next

7. Select your goals (choose what matters to you):
   - Generate leads
   - Drive online sales
   - etc.
   - Click Create

8. Accept the terms of service

9. Set up a Web Data Stream:
   - Click "Web"
   - Enter your website URL: https://travelwebapp-pied.vercel.app
   - Stream name: "Wander Web"
   - Click "Create stream"

10. IMPORTANT - Copy your Measurement ID:
    - You'll see something like: G-XXXXXXXXXX
    - This is your unique tracking code
    - Copy it!


STEP 2: ADD THE TRACKING ID TO YOUR PROJECT
-------------------------------------------
Your project has a file called ".env.local" in the root folder.
This file stores secret/private settings that shouldn't be shared publicly.

Option A: If .env.local already exists
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
1. Open the file: C:\Users\LENOVO\Desktop\New folder\Projects\Travelwebapp\wander\.env.local

2. Add this line at the bottom (replace G-XXXXXXXXXX with YOUR actual ID):

   NEXT_PUBLIC_GA_MEASUREMENT_ID=G-XXXXXXXXXX

3. Save the file


Option B: If .env.local doesn't exist
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
1. Create a new file called ".env.local" in:
   C:\Users\LENOVO\Desktop\New folder\Projects\Travelwebapp\wander\

2. Copy everything from ".env.example" into it

3. Add this line (replace G-XXXXXXXXXX with YOUR actual ID):

   NEXT_PUBLIC_GA_MEASUREMENT_ID=G-XXXXXXXXXX

4. Save the file


STEP 3: DEPLOY TO VERCEL
------------------------
If you're using Vercel for hosting, you also need to add the variable there:

1. Go to: https://vercel.com/dashboard

2. Click on your "wander" project

3. Go to "Settings" tab

4. Click "Environment Variables" in the left sidebar

5. Add a new variable:
   - Name: NEXT_PUBLIC_GA_MEASUREMENT_ID
   - Value: G-XXXXXXXXXX (your actual ID)
   - Environment: Production (and Preview if you want)

6. Click "Save"

7. Redeploy your site (or it will apply on next deployment)


HOW TO VERIFY IT'S WORKING
--------------------------
1. After deploying, visit your live website

2. Go back to Google Analytics

3. Click "Reports" in the left sidebar

4. Click "Realtime"

5. You should see yourself as an active user!

Note: Regular reports take 24-48 hours to populate with data.


WHAT DATA WILL YOU SEE?
-----------------------
Once set up, Google Analytics shows you:

- How many people visit your site
- Which pages are most popular
- Where visitors come from (Google, social media, direct)
- What countries/cities your visitors are in
- What devices they use (mobile, desktop)
- How long they stay on your site
- Conversion tracking (if you set up goals)


TROUBLESHOOTING
---------------
Q: I added the ID but don't see any data
A: Wait 24-48 hours. Also check that:
   - The ID starts with "G-" (not "UA-" which is old)
   - You restarted your development server after adding the env variable
   - You redeployed to Vercel after adding the variable there

Q: Where do I find my Measurement ID again?
A: Google Analytics > Admin (gear icon) > Data Streams > Click your stream > Measurement ID

Q: Is this free?
A: Yes, Google Analytics is completely free.


PRIVACY NOTE
------------
If you're collecting data from EU visitors, you should:
1. Add a cookie consent banner to your site
2. Only load analytics after user consents
3. Mention analytics in your Privacy Policy

This is already mentioned in your /privacy page but you may want to add
a cookie consent popup for full GDPR compliance.
